You can navigate the entire site using just your keyboard, and it works great with screen readers like JAWS, NVDA, and VoiceOver. **Last Updated: January 2025**

Gather Pics is committed to providing an accessible digital experience for all users, regardless of their abilities or disabilities. We strive to ensure that our Service is usable by everyone, including individuals who use assistive technologies.

---

## 1. Accessibility Standard

This site has been built in accordance with the **Israeli Standard 5568** (Accessibility Requirements for Internet Content) at the level of **WCAG 2.1 Level AA**.

## 2. Accessibility Features

We have implemented various technical and design measures to ensure the site is accessible:

### Navigation & Keyboard Support
* **Full Keyboard Control**: The site can be navigated entirely using a keyboard (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Esc).
* **Gallery Navigation**: Users can navigate through image galleries using arrow keys (Left/Right/Up/Down).
* **Focus Indicators**: Clear visual focus rings help users identify their current position on the page.
* **Skip Links**: Mechanism to bypass repetitive content and jump directly to the main area.

### Visual & Content
* **Contrast**: We adhere to strict color contrast ratios to ensure text is readable.
* **Text Resizing**: The site supports browser-based zooming up to 200% without loss of functionality.
* **Structure**: The site uses semantic HTML markers (Headings, Landmarks, Lists) to help screen readers understand the page structure.

### Image Accessibility (Alt Text)
* **Auto-Generation**: Our system automatically generates basic technical descriptions for images to assist screen readers (e.g., identifying photo index and context).
* **Manual Descriptions**: We provide tools for photographers to add detailed, custom descriptions to their photos.

## 3. Supported Technologies

The Service is designed to work with major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and assistive technologies, including screen readers such as **NVDA**, **JAWS**, **VoiceOver**, and **TalkBack**.

## 4. Known Limitations (User-Generated Content)

Please note that Gather Pics is a platform for hosting content created by third parties (Photographers). While we provide the accessible infrastructure:
* **Image Descriptions**: The quality and existence of detailed alternative text (Alt Text) depend on the Photographer uploading the content.
* **Uploaded Files**: Specific images or files uploaded by users may not be fully accessible.

We actively encourage our community of photographers to make their galleries accessible to everyone.
