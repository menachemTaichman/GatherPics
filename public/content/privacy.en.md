## 1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Service:

### A. Account Information (Photographers/Subscribers)

When you register for an account, we collect:
- **Name/Label**: Your display name or business name
- **Email Address**: Used for account communication and login
- **Password**: Stored in encrypted form (we cannot see your actual password)
- **Account Preferences**: User interface settings and preferences stored locally and on our servers

### B. Event and Content Data

When you create events and upload content, we collect:
- **Event Information**: Event names, dates, URLs, visibility settings, and metadata
- **Images and Videos**: All photos and videos you upload to the Service
- **Image Metadata**: File sizes, dimensions, dates taken, descriptions, and technical information
- **Albums and Moments**: Organizational structures you create (albums, moments, collections)

### C. Biometric Data (Face Recognition)

**This is a critical section – please read carefully.**

When you upload images, our Service automatically processes biometric data:
- **Facial Recognition Data**: We use AWS Rekognition to detect and analyze faces in your images
- **Face Identifiers**: Unique identifiers assigned to detected faces
- **Face Groupings**: Clustering of similar faces to help organize photos by person
- **Face Coordinates**: Bounding box information showing where faces appear in images

**Important Notes:**
- Biometric data is stored in AWS Rekognition collections (one per event)
- This data is processed automatically when images are uploaded
- The data is used solely for organizing and discovering photos within your events
- Biometric data is deleted when an event is deleted
- We do not use biometric data for any purpose other than organizing your event galleries

### D. Usage and Technical Data

We automatically collect certain information when you use the Service:
- **Device Information**: Browser type, device type, operating system
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent on the Service
- **IP Address**: Collected for security and technical purposes (stored with refresh tokens)
- **User Agent**: Browser and device information (collected with authentication tokens)
- **Session Data**: Information stored in your browser's session storage for functionality

### E. Communication Data

When you contact us or use certain features, we may collect:
- **Feedback Messages**: If you submit feedback, we collect your message and optionally your name and email
- **Access Requests**: If you request access to an event, we collect your name and email address
- **Communication Preferences**: Whether you consent to receive communications from us

### F. Payment Information

If you subscribe to paid services:
- **Payment Processing**: Payment information is processed by third-party payment processors
- **We do not store full credit card numbers or payment details on our servers**
- Payment processors handle all financial transactions securely

## 3. Data Controller vs. Data Processor

**Important Legal Distinction:**

### For Photographers (Subscribers)
- **You are the Data Controller** for all content you upload, including images and biometric data of individuals appearing in your photos
- **We are the Data Processor** – we process your data on your behalf to provide the Service
- **You are responsible for:**
  - Obtaining consent from individuals whose images and biometric data are processed
  - Complying with all applicable privacy laws (GDPR, Israeli Privacy Law, etc.)
  - Ensuring you have the legal right to process biometric data
  - Managing access permissions and data sharing

### For Event Guests and End Users
- If you are viewing an event as a guest, the Photographer (Event Owner) is the Data Controller
- We process your viewing activity and any information you provide (like access requests) as a Data Processor on behalf of the Photographer
- For questions about how your data is handled, please contact the Photographer who created the event
