**Quick Summary:** Gather Pics is a professional gallery platform designed specifically for professional photographers and photography businesses. It uses AI to automatically organize your event photos by recognizing faces, helping you create beautiful, secure galleries for your clients while giving you complete control over whe sees what. Perfect for professionals who want to save time on sorting and deliver a premium experience to their clients.

**Intelligent Galleries for Professional Photographers**

Gather Pics combines powerful AI face recognition with intuitive organization tools, allowing you to create beautiful, secure, and searchable event galleries. We automate the sorting process so you can focus on delivering exceptional photo experiences.

---

## Core Features

### 🤖 Smart Face Recognition
Powered by **AWS Rekognition**, our platform automates the tedious work of sorting photos:
- **Auto-Detection** – Identifies faces in uploaded photos instantly.
- **Person Grouping** – Automatically organizes photos by individual, making specific guests easy to find.
- **Smart Discovery** – Helps clients and guests find themselves and their loved ones effortlessly.

### 🔒 Advanced Permission System
We provide complete control over content access per photo, per album, and per person:
- **Granular Control** – Customize permissions at every level.
- **Identity Privacy** – For person-based access, you can manage identity visibility independently of photo access. This ensures that users can view an image based on general permissions, but will remain unable to see associated face tags unless explicitly authorized.
- **Secure Enforcement** – All permissions are enforced strictly at the database level.

### 👥 Client Collaboration
Empower your clients to actively participate:
- **Photo Selection** – Let event owners review and select their top picks.
- **Custom Albums** – Give clients the freedom to create their own collections.
- **Easy Downloads** – Offer simple, high-quality download options.
- **Face Group Access** – Allow guests to request access to specific people, unlocking the ability to identify and filter photos of themselves.

### 📊 Business & Workflow
- **Manage Multiple Events** – Handle unlimited events and clients from a single dashboard.
- **Flexible Organization** – Organize by Albums, Time-based Moments, or Face Groups.
- **Modern Interface** – A fast, responsive design that works beautifully on any device (Mobile/Desktop).
