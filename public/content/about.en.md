## Key Features

### 🤖 Smart Face Recognition

Our platform uses advanced AI face recognition technology (powered by AWS Rekognition) to automatically:

- **Detect Faces** – Automatically identify faces in all your uploaded photos
- **Group by Person** – Organize photos by person, making it easy to find all photos of a specific individual
- **Discover Connections** – Help clients discover photos of themselves and their loved ones effortlessly
- **Save Time** – Eliminate hours of manual photo sorting and tagging

### 📁 Flexible Organization

Organize your photos in multiple ways:

- **Albums** – Create custom albums for different purposes (ceremony, reception, portraits, etc.)
- **Moments** – Organize photos by time periods or event segments
- **Face Groups** – Automatically organized collections of photos by person
- **Custom Collections** – Build any organizational structure that works for your workflow

### 🔒 Advanced Permission System

Gather Pics features a sophisticated, multi-layered permission system that gives you complete control over who can see and do what:

**Event-Level Permissions:**
- **Management Rights** – Control who can manage event settings, add users, and configure permissions
- **Content Rights** – Set who can upload, delete, and edit photos
- **Viewing Rights** – Determine who can view the event and what content they can see

**Granular Content Control:**
- **Full Access or Selective** – Grant users access to all content, or restrict them to specific albums, moments, or face groups
- **Image-Level Permissions** – Control access down to individual images
- **Group-Based Access** – Allow guests to see only photos of specific people (face groups)
- **Album Restrictions** – Limit access to certain albums while keeping others private

**User Management:**
- **Hierarchical System** – Multi-level permission hierarchy for managing teams and assistants
- **Event-Specific Profiles** – Create profiles restricted to specific events
- **Public Guest Access** – Generate secure access codes for event guests without requiring full accounts
- **Access Requests** – Guests can request access to specific photo groups, which you can approve or deny

**Database-Enforced Security:**
- All permissions are enforced at the database level, ensuring security and consistency
- Permissions are automatically applied across all views and operations
- No content can be accessed without proper authorization

This powerful system allows you to create custom permission structures for any scenario – from giving full access to event owners, to restricting guests to only their own photos, to managing team members with different levels of responsibility.
